# 第三期静帧生成 Prompts

适用：

- `image2`
- `GPT Image`
- 其他偏电影感静帧模型

## 使用原则

这期不要一条 prompt 直接做完整片。

更稳的方式是：

1. 先做 `6 张静帧`
2. 前 `1-4` 镜是严肃雪山机甲片
3. 后 `5-6` 镜是片场穿帮和收工
4. 再决定哪些镜头做图生视频

## 重要说明

如果模型对明确 IP 名称不稳定，内部可先用：

- `红发机甲女驾驶员`
- `红色二号机风格生体机甲`

先把镜头关系做对，再看要不要往更明确的角色感上推。

## 镜头1：雪山大全景

### 目标

- 一眼像严肃机甲短片
- 先把观众骗进去
- 远处能看到二号机轮廓

### 可选备用 Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
A vast frozen mountain landscape during a violent snowstorm. In the distance, partially buried in snow and ice, stands the silhouette of a giant red biomechanical mecha unit, damaged and motionless. A small red-haired female mecha pilot is visible in the foreground or lower midground, moving across the snow toward it.

Visual goals:
- serious, cold, emotional, apocalyptic
- massive scale difference between the girl and the mecha
- strong wind, snow haze, frozen atmosphere
- the mecha should feel like a legendary lost machine half-buried in the mountain
- no comedy yet
- no hint that this is a film set yet

Style:
- premium theatrical sci-fi animation
- cold blue-gray palette
- cinematic snow lighting
- epic scale
- emotionally heavy
- high-end 3D materials
- film still quality

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- wide establishing shot
- pilot small, mecha distant but readable
- strong loneliness and scale

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no crew
- no set reveal
- keep it serious
```

## 镜头2：肩后跟进镜头

### 目标

- 比开场更近
- 让观众开始跟着她看
- 机体仍在前方，尺度还在

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
From behind the red-haired female mecha pilot, the camera looks past her shoulder toward the buried red mecha in the snowy distance. She is closer to camera now, wearing heavy winter clothing, while the giant machine remains ahead in the storm.

Visual goals:
- serious and emotional
- strong wind and cold
- over-the-shoulder point-of-view feeling
- the audience should feel closer to her now
- no comedy
- no set reveal
- this should feel like the middle of a tragic sci-fi rescue scene

Style:
- premium theatrical sci-fi animation
- cold blue-gray palette
- dramatic snowstorm
- realistic fabric, armor, and snow detail
- film still quality

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- medium-long over-the-shoulder shot
- pilot occupies foreground as back / half-body silhouette
- mecha visible ahead in mid-distance
- strong continuity from the opening shot

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no comedy
- no crew
```

## 镜头3：侧脸抬头反应

### 目标

- 只拍人物反应
- 侧脸缓缓抬头
- 不拍机体
- 把气氛推到高潮前一秒

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
The red-haired female mecha pilot has stopped in the snow. In side profile, she slowly lifts her head and looks upward out of frame. Snow blows across her face and winter hood. This is a restrained emotional reaction shot, not a reveal of the mecha.

Visual goals:
- emotional climax before the reveal
- quiet recognition and tension
- strong side-profile identity
- still completely serious
- still no sign that this is fake

Style:
- premium theatrical sci-fi animation
- cold storm light
- epic scale
- moody, dramatic, heavy
- high-end cinematic 3D look

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- medium shot or medium close-up
- clean side-profile silhouette
- gaze directed upward out of frame
- character dominates the frame

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no comedy
- no behind-the-scenes elements
```

## 镜头4：手握插入栓舱门

### 目标

- 反转前最后一张严肃图
- 只拍手、舱门、袖口
- 下一秒就要发生什么，但还没被打断

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
A human hand in a red winter sleeve grips the cold metallic entry plug hatch / cockpit hatch of the giant red mecha. Frost and snow cling to the surface. This is the final tense insert shot before the scene is suddenly interrupted.

Visual goals:
- intense emotional pause
- the audience should think the real story is about to begin
- no comedy
- no behind-the-scenes reveal yet

Style:
- premium theatrical sci-fi animation
- cold, dramatic light
- elegant cinematic detail
- serious, high-budget feeling

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- close-up or medium close-up insert shot
- hand and hatch dominate the frame
- strong tactile tension before action

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no crew
- no foam
- no cardboard
- no set edges
```

## 镜头5：喊咔，片场穿帮

### 目标

- 反转镜头
- 同一雪山突然显得很假
- 观众一眼看懂：这其实是片场

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
The same snowy mountain mecha scene is suddenly revealed to be a low-budget film set. The fake snow is clearly foam. Wind is being created by hairdryers or blower fans. The giant red mecha is obviously a cardboard and scaffold prop with exposed seams, tape, support structures, and unfinished backside materials. The atmosphere has just broken as if someone shouted “cut.”

Visual goals:
- sudden tonal collapse from epic sci-fi to absurd handmade film set
- foam snow chunks visible
- blower fans or hairdryers visible
- cardboard mecha prop visible
- set edges, support rods, tape, and backstage logic visible
- the reveal should be funny at a glance

Style:
- still cinematic and polished
- but now obviously handmade, low-budget, and absurd
- warm light starts mixing into the cold snow set
- strong visual contrast with the serious earlier shots

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- keep enough of the mecha and snow set to show the fake reveal
- readable behind-the-scenes chaos

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- do not make it dirty or ugly
- it should feel like a lovingly terrible set
```

## 镜头6：派遣所小黄人收工

### 目标

- 派遣所人格正式出现
- 小黄人举牌 `没有 token 了`
- 大家开始慌乱收景

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
The fake snowy mountain set is now full of tiny yellow computer-worker robots from the dispatch office rushing in to shut everything down. Some are holding signs that say “没有 token 了”. Some are dragging cardboard prop panels. Some are turning off blower fans. Some are stuffing foam snow into bags. Some are carrying tape, clipboards, and props while panicking and trying to wrap the shoot. In the distant background, the red-haired actress remains in character for a second too long, standing small in the snow with her hands on her hips or stomping in frustration because the scene was interrupted.

Tone:
- absurd
- frantic
- funny
- low-budget film crew collapse
- very busy but not truly competent

Visual goals:
- the robots should feel like a film crew from 人工智障派遣所
- one or two signs with “没有 token 了” should be clearly visible
- cardboard mecha, foam snow, and fake wind machines should still be visible
- the whole set should feel like it is being torn down too early
- the actress should be a small background gag, not the focus
- her anger should read as sincere frustration, not slapstick

Style:
- premium theatrical family-animation comedy
- polished toy-like 3D materials
- controlled comic chaos
- cinematic lighting

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- wide enough to show both set and crew
- readable at a glance
- foreground is busy crew teardown
- background contains the tiny angry actress as a secondary joke

Important:
- no humans except the actress if needed in background
- signs can contain simple Chinese text: 没有 token 了
- no watermark
- funny but not ugly
```

## 补充说明

如果片长只允许 `6 镜`，建议把：

- 穿帮后的撤场群像
- `没有 token 了`
- 明日香后景生气
- 片场收口信息

都合并在 `镜头6` 里，不再单独切第七镜。

### Prompt

```text
Use the white robot director identity only as a calm command-center figure if included.

Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
Inside or beside the collapsing fake snow set, the final meta reveal lands on the dispatch-office film production logic. The white robot director or a dispatch control station stands in the foreground, calm and in control, while the yellow worker robots continue hurriedly striking the set in the background. A handmade sign or board can read “没有 token 了” or “先收工”. Farther back in the snow set, the red-haired actress is still visibly annoyed, standing off to the side with hands on hips as everyone else ignores her and keeps wrapping.

Visual goals:
- this is the final joke and brand identity shot
- the chaos is still happening, but the frame is more composed
- the white robot director should feel calm, steady, and slightly absurdly professional
- the whole thing should land as a premium animated comedy brand shot
- the ignored angry actress in the deep background should add one last layer of comedy

Style:
- premium theatrical family animation
- cinematic comedy ending
- polished toy-like materials
- warm/cool mixed lighting from set and backstage
- strong foreground/background separation

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- clear ending frame
- good negative space for future title placement

Important:
- simple Chinese signage is allowed if needed:
  - 没有 token 了
  - 先收工
- no watermark
- no ugly clutter
- end on a polished but ridiculous note
```

## 优先顺序

如果你要先跑最关键的 3 张，建议先做：

1. `镜头4：手握插入栓舱门`
2. `镜头5：喊咔，片场穿帮`
3. `镜头6：派遣所小黄人收工`

因为这三张最能决定笑点和反转成不成立。
