# 第三期角色定妆 Prompts

这期先不要急着跑完整分镜。

最稳的顺序是：

1. 先锁 `明日香真人版` 的脸和服装
2. 再锁 `二号机机头` 的严肃版
3. 再做 `二号机机头纸壳片场版`
4. 最后再回去做整条剧情分镜

## 总原则

前半段要像：

- 真人电影
- 冷峻雪山
- 正经末世机甲片

后半段才露出：

- 纸壳机头
- 泡沫雪
- 小黄人片场

所以角色照也要分成两套：

- `正片定妆`
- `片场穿帮定妆`

---

## 1. 明日香真人定妆照

### 目标

- 纯真人
- 越像电影剧照越好
- 不要动漫脸
- 不要卡通渲染
- 红发、冷感、末世感

### Prompt

```text
Create a hyper-real cinematic live-action character portrait in vertical 9:16.

Subject:
A realistic young red-haired female mecha pilot standing in a cold snow-mountain environment. She should feel like a live-action film actress, not animation, not illustration, not cosplay photo, not anime render.

Appearance:
- realistic human face
- natural skin texture
- subtle cold wind on face and hair
- red hair, slightly messy from snow and wind
- intense, serious, emotionally guarded expression
- no exaggerated anime proportions
- no cartoon features

Wardrobe:
- fitted red or red-accented pilot suit / battle suit
- practical sci-fi details
- slightly weathered, cold, snow-dusted

Tone:
- serious
- cinematic
- emotionally heavy
- post-apocalyptic mecha drama

Style:
- photoreal live-action
- premium blockbuster film still
- realistic lens depth
- cold blue-gray mountain light
- subtle snow particles
- high-end color grading

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- medium close-up or waist-up portrait
- actress dominant in frame
- snow mountain atmosphere in background

Important:
- must look like a real actress in a real film
- no anime style
- no cartoon rendering
- no text
- no watermark
```

---

## 2. 明日香雪山全身 / 行进定妆

### 目标

- 同一个真人脸
- 带动作姿态
- 方便后面接雪山找机体的镜头

### Prompt

```text
Create a hyper-real cinematic live-action full-body character still in vertical 9:16.

Subject:
The same realistic red-haired female mecha pilot walking through deep snow in a mountain storm. She should look like the lead actress of a serious live-action sci-fi film.

Visual goals:
- fully realistic human
- determined body language
- moving forward through strong wind and snow
- practical futuristic suit
- serious and emotionally intense

Style:
- photoreal live-action
- premium film still
- cold mountain palette
- realistic snow and wind
- dramatic but grounded

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- full body or medium-full shot
- strong forward motion
- believable snow interaction

Important:
- keep the same face identity as the portrait version
- no anime style
- no cartoon look
- no text
- no watermark
```

## 3. 二号机机头严肃版

### 目标

- 只先锁机头 / 上半部分
- 前半段要像真的机甲电影道具或高成本机甲
- 不能一上来像纸壳

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic close-up of the head and upper-neck area of a giant red biomechanical mecha unit in vertical 9:16.

Subject:
The head of a damaged red biomechanical mecha emerging from snow and ice in a mountain storm. It should feel large, serious, iconic, and emotionally heavy.

Visual goals:
- intimidating mecha head silhouette
- realistic damage, ice, scratches, snow buildup
- red primary color with darker mechanical details
- no toy look
- no cardboard look
- no obvious fake set elements

Style:
- high-end cinematic sci-fi
- realistic materials
- cold storm lighting
- dramatic, moody, serious
- blockbuster film still quality

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- close-up on head and upper structure
- strong vertical presence
- snow and wind moving around the machine

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no crew
- no foam
- no tape
- no cardboard seams
```

---

## 4. 二号机机头片场纸壳版

### 目标

- 和上一张保持同一个机头设计语言
- 但一眼能看出是纸壳道具
- 方便后面做穿帮反转

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic behind-the-scenes version of the same giant red mecha head prop in vertical 9:16.

Subject:
The same red biomechanical mecha head, but now clearly revealed as a low-budget practical film-set prop made from cardboard, painted panels, support rods, tape, foam, and rough handmade construction.

Visual goals:
- same general mecha design as the serious version
- clearly fake and handmade on closer look
- visible cardboard edges, seams, tape, brackets, support structure
- still charming and cinematic, not ugly
- should feel like a lovingly terrible set prop

Style:
- cinematic comedy reveal
- practical low-budget prop aesthetic
- warm backstage light mixed with cold snow-set light
- readable and funny

Composition:
- vertical 9:16
- similar framing to the serious head version if possible
- enough detail to read the fake construction

Important:
- no text
- no watermark
- no humans unless tiny blurred crew in background
- keep it visually connected to the serious version
```

---

## 5. 推荐先跑顺序

建议先跑：

1. `明日香真人定妆照`
2. `二号机机头严肃版`
3. `二号机机头片场纸壳版`

原因很简单：

- 先锁人脸
- 再锁机头设计
- 最后再做反转版

这样后面整条片子最不容易散。
