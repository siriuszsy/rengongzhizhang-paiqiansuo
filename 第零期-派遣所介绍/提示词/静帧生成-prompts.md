# 第零期静帧生成 Prompts

## 使用方式

- 建议模型：`GPT Image / image2`
- 画幅：`9:16`
- 每个镜头先出 `4 张`
- 统一参考图：
  - 所长参考：`素材池/头像/ChatGPT Image 2026年4月27日 21_38_38.png`
- 先做静帧，不做字幕，不做 logo，不做文字

## 统一总控风格词

每一张都尽量保留这些风格：

- `premium theatrical family animation`
- `chaotic office comedy`
- `toy-like 3D materials`
- `warm cinematic lighting`
- `clear silhouette staging`
- `controlled comic chaos`
- `yellow capsule-shaped computer worker robots`
- `calm, authoritative white robot director`
- `polished 3D materials`
- `production-quality animated key visual`

不要直接写：

- Disney
- Pixar
- Minions

## 镜头 1：纸团陨石砸桌

### 目标

做出一个一眼能看懂的开场钩子：

- 巨大纸团像陨石
- 即将砸到桌面
- 荒诞，但不是灾难片
- 是办公室喜剧

### Prompt

```text
Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
A gigantic crumpled paper ball, like a meteor made of office paperwork, is crashing down toward the director’s desk inside the dispatch office headquarters. The moment is dramatic, funny, and absurd, but still clearly set inside a whimsical office world.

Visual requirements:
- The paper ball is huge, exaggerated, and feels heavy
- It is seconds away from impact with the desk
- Sheets, sticky notes, and tiny paper fragments fly around it
- The office lighting is warm and theatrical, not realistic disaster lighting
- The image should feel like the opening shot of a high-end animated comedy short
- Controlled chaos, not total destruction
- Beautiful cinematic depth of field
- Toy-like premium 3D materials
- Clear silhouette and readable action

World:
- This is the office of a fictional company called 派遣所
- The environment should hint at a dispatch office run by tiny robot employees
- Background can include blurred yellow micro-computer worker robots reacting to the incoming paper meteor
- The whole scene should feel busy, humorous, and expensive

Important:
- No text
- No logo
- No watermark
- No direct imitation of existing copyrighted animated films or characters
- Keep it original, polished, and production-ready
```

- 局面热闹、可爱、可笑

### Prompt

```text
Use the uploaded white robot director image as a world reference for the dispatch office style.

Create a premium cinematic 3D animated keyframe in vertical 9:16.

Scene:
The dispatch office suddenly comes alive after a new assignment arrives. Tiny yellow capsule-shaped computer worker robots rush around their desks, screens flash, paper files fly, small devices light up, and everyone looks extremely busy.

But the comedy is:
- They look energetic and committed
- They are clearly trying very hard
- Yet the overall vibe suggests they are not especially competent

Visual elements:
- Small employee robots with tablets, tiny keyboards, papers, office gadgets, headsets
- Some running in the wrong direction
- Some bumping into desks
- Some lifting too many files
- Tiny glowing monitors and dispatch boards
- Warm, premium office lighting
- Controlled comic chaos

Style:
- High-end theatrical family animation
- Busy office comedy
- Premium toy-like 3D materials
- Rich warm color palette
- Cinematic depth and staging

Important:
- No text
- No logo
- No watermark
- Keep the image readable and staged, not messy noise
```
